The source is a training course's slides, not a book. Each slide is a title
and a few terse lines, and the text below gives this section's slides one
after another. Explain what those lines mean, in connected prose that covers
every slide, instead of repeating them as a list. A slide that is a command,
a configuration fragment or a recipe belongs in the code section, whole.
A diagram's labels reach you as a jumble of names, one or a few to a line (a
dependency graph, a boot sequence, a block diagram): describe what the
diagram shows only as far as the slides' text says it, and never list its
labels. A "Practical lab" slide only announces a lab; name the lab and add
nothing else. The hard rules below hold here as for a book: what the slides
do not say, the note does not say either.
